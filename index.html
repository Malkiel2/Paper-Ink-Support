Paper Ink

A little ink. A clever fold.

Place a drop on your half of the paper. Fold, press and open to transfer the ink onto the opposite half. Cover the opposing drawings before the computer covers yours.

Help and support

For help, questions or bug reports, email malkiel1234@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Screenshots are optional; please remove any personal information.

Your progress

Scores, stars and settings are saved locally on your device. No account or internet connection is needed to play. Uninstalling the app may delete its local progress.

Controls

Drag your blue drawings before starting. Tap for a Small ink drop, or hold briefly for a Large drop. Drag to aim and release to fold the paper. Pause using the button above the sheet. Sound, haptics and gentler motion can be changed in Settings.
