Privacy Policy

Paper Ink for iPhone and iPad
Effective: 14 October 2026

No data collection by the game

Paper Ink operates offline. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data from the game. The game has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, chat, in-app purchases or third-party data collection services.

Local storage

Game progress, scores, stars and preferences are stored on your device. These are not sent to the developer. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and Apple account settings. Uninstalling the app may delete local progress.

Device permissions

The game does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Support correspondence

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue; it is not collected automatically by the game. Please do not send sensitive information.

This website and Apple services

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose service may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website uses no developer-added analytics or tracking scripts. Apple independently operates the App Store and related services under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal data from players of any age.
