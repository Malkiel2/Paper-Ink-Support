Privacy Policy

Paper Ink for iPhone, iPad and Android
Updated: 26 October 2026

Which version this covers

The original offline store release has no game analytics, advertising, purchases or online player accounts. Newer versions that include Friends and gameplay analytics work as described below. Features available to you depend on the installed version. Version 1.0.1 introduces additional paper worlds, characters and notebook content, optional ad-supported paper trials, interstitial advertising and a purchase removing interstitials. These features do not appear in older versions. The game has no player chat. Purchases use the stores and RevenueCat as described below.

Progress on your device

Campaign progress, scores, stars and preferences remain stored on your device. We do not upload your campaign save to Firebase. Uninstalling the app may remove local progress. Operating-system backups are controlled by your device and account settings.

Playing with a friend

Online Friends is optional and requires an internet connection. Same-device Friends works offline and creates no server room. It uses Google Firebase to maintain a private two-player room, validate turns and deliver the shared game state. The service processes temporary game positions, ink marks, turn/results, a room invitation code and randomly generated session identifiers. You do not need to create a Firebase or other player account. Session authorization secrets stay private; share only the invitation code/link with your intended friend.

When a series ends, the main board is removed. Each private final-board copy is removed after that player receives the result; disconnected copies are eligible for background cleanup after one minute. Small session/result metadata is retained briefly to support a rematch, normally expiring after five minutes. Unjoined invitations expire after ten minutes and inactive joined rooms after one hour. Expired data is removed by bounded periodic cleanup; cleanup delays and service failures can extend storage beyond these eligibility times. Leaving on both devices removes the room. Cloud providers may retain separate technical logs or backups under their policies.

App verification and service security

Firebase App Check uses Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity to help verify legitimate apps. Our server also processes technical request information such as IP addresses for abuse prevention and uses hashed IP-based rate-limit keys. Firebase/Google Cloud and Apple or Google verification services may process device/app integrity and network information. These security operations are separate from gameplay analytics.

Gameplay analytics

Current normal Release versions automatically report gameplay statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase. There is no in-game Analytics switch or permission popup. Earlier development versions had an optional Settings preference; that preference is not used in current versions. Debug, automated QA and store-capture builds do not upload Analytics events.

Google Analytics receives app/device and engagement information, a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, screen names and gameplay summaries such as selected world/level, mode, difficulty, attempts, wins/losses, stars, score, fold totals, tutorial use, connection issues, rematch choices and bounded purchase-offer/checkout/restore outcomes. We use these statistics to improve clarity, enjoyment, balance and reliability and guide product decisions. SDK batching and connectivity affect delivery.

We do not send drawings, board positions, ink polygons, room codes, invitation links, session secrets, names or email addresses in Analytics events. Gameplay Analytics does not automatically collect advertising identifiers or Apple IDFV. Advertising and optional campaign-measurement services have separate privacy controls described below. We do not add a player user ID or connect events to a player login. Google applies the property's configured retention rules and its service policies. Contact us at the address below with a privacy request.

Optional purchases

Supported versions offer a one-time purchase opening the first ten campaign worlds, which remain earnable through play, and additional permanent worlds, cosmetic characters and removal of interstitial advertising. Apple or Google processes payment; RevenueCat verifies transactions and supplies purchased-access status. The purchase SDK creates a pseudonymous customer identifier and processes purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, app/device metadata and network information needed to deliver, restore and maintain access. Automatic device-identifier collection and purchase-SDK diagnostics are disabled. We do not receive payment-card details and do not put receipts or RevenueCat customer identifiers in gameplay Analytics. Purchase verification is separate from gameplay analytics and does not require a Firebase player account. Restore purchases uses the same store account and platform; it does not restore local gameplay progress. Store refunds and revocations may remove paid access without deleting earned progress.

Advertising inside the game (version 1.0.1)

We use Google AdMob and its disclosed advertising providers for interstitial ads between eligible single-player duels and optional rewarded ads. We do not display banner ads. A rewarded ad offers exactly one Free Play duel on the selected paid paper; it does not grant permanent ownership, campaign progress, a character or a notebook cover. The permanent No interstitial ads purchase removes automatic interstitials; optional reward videos remain your choice. An ad may be unavailable.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device/app identifiers (including advertising identifiers where permitted), ad views and interactions, app/product interactions, crash information, diagnostics and performance information. These support ad delivery, personalization where allowed, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising data may be shared with the advertising providers identified in the consent interface and Google's provider information. Network data is encrypted in transit. We do not provide precise location, drawings or private room codes to advertising SDKs.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents a privacy message explaining purposes and providers and offering consent choices. You can revisit the applicable Ad privacy options in Settings. Ad requests and the available personalized, non-personalized or limited modes depend on those choices and applicable requirements. Refusing tracking or personalization does not remove gameplay, purchases or earned progress. No reward is offered for granting a privacy permission.

On iPhone and iPad, cross-company tracking also requires Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization. We reuse the app's existing system permission state. You can manage it in device Settings. Regional advertising consent does not automatically authorize separate Meta/TikTok campaign measurement. Device advertising-ID controls are also available in Android settings.

Measuring campaigns for Paper Ink

We may advertise Paper Ink through Apple Ads, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok. When a provider is configured and you explicitly agree to campaign measurement, limited app activation, tutorial/level achievement, product view, checkout and verified purchase events may be shared with that provider. These SDKs may process app/device information, identifiers where permitted, IP address, operating-system/language information and event timestamps. On iOS, tracking additionally requires ATT. Bundled campaign-measurement defaults are disabled until configured; no previous game-event history is replayed when you consent.

You can withdraw the separate Ad measurement choice in Settings when that feature is configured. Withdrawal stops new forwarding; it does not erase data already received by a provider. Some SDK changes complete on the next app launch. Apple privacy-preserving attribution may be used separately, subject to Apple's eligibility and aggregation rules. Google Analytics may receive a verified purchase product/value/currency and a hashed transaction reference for funnel measurement; receipts and payment-card data are not sent in these events.

Permissions and sharing

The game does not request contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone access. Sharing an invitation opens your operating system's share sheet. You select the app/recipient and decide whether to send it. Links prefill a room code; they do not automatically join a game. The invitation website has no developer-added advertising or Analytics scripts.

Service providers

Google provides Firebase, Google Analytics and Google Cloud under its privacy policy. RevenueCat provides purchase verification and entitlement services under its privacy policy. Apple and Google independently provide their stores, integrity services and device backups under their policies. For advertising delivery and campaign measurement, see the separate sections above and Google advertising information, Meta privacy and TikTok business products terms. Advertising-related disclosures and any applicable opt-out rights are presented through the regional privacy interface.

Support correspondence and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you choose to send, to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send sensitive information. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages (with an additional static Firebase Hosting support/verification page). These hosting services may process technical requests. GitHub Pages may process technical requests under GitHub's privacy statement. It uses no developer-added tracking scripts.

Age groups and privacy choices

Before in-game ads are enabled, Paper Ink asks for an age group, without asking for a birthday, name, contact information or account. The choice is stored on your device and can be changed in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can choose not to share an age group. The app supplies regulatory ad-treatment signals to Google rather than a birth date or exact age.

For users who select an age below 18 or choose not to share it, we use Google's conservative child ad treatment: personalized ads and remarketing are disabled, advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) are not transmitted by the ad SDK, and third-party ad pixels are disabled. We do not request Apple tracking permission or forward campaign events to Meta/TikTok for these groups. Gameplay and purchases remain available. No reward is offered for selecting a particular age group or granting consent. Adult ad treatment still depends on applicable regional choices and Apple tracking permission.

If you have a privacy request or believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us. The game is not enrolled in Apple's Made for Kids program.
